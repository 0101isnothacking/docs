---
title: "Email discovery"
description: "Find and validate email addresses, and check breach data during passive reconnaissance."
---

Discover likely email addresses for a target organization and validate them with low-noise checks.

<Warning>
  Only use these techniques for authorized engagements. Handle any breach or credential data responsibly and legally.
</Warning>

## Find addresses

- [Hunter.io email finder](https://hunter.io/email-finder)
- [Phonebook.cz](https://phonebook.cz/)
- [Clearbit extension](https://extensionstores.com/extension/clearbit/)

## Validate addresses

- [Email Checker](https://email-checker.net/)
- [Email Hippo](https://tools.emailhippo.com/)
- [Google account recovery](https://accounts.google.com) can reveal partial details via the forgot-password flow

<Note>
  Do not trust one result alone. Build a shortlist from common company naming patterns, compare it across multiple discovery tools, and validate it with low-noise checks. A likely address becomes much more reliable when the same pattern shows up in public sources, password reset hints, or breach data.
</Note>

## Breach and leak data

Breach compilations and leak-lookup services can surface known addresses and reused password hashes. A shared password hash can link one address to another account.

- [Dehashed](https://app.dehashed.com/search)
- [Have I Been Pwned](https://haveibeenpwned.com)
- [WeLeakInfo](https://weleakinfo.org/)
- [LeakCheck](https://leakcheck.io/)
